About

Performance numbers by profession, light by obsession.

I'm a Senior Test Engineer at AMD with four-plus years validating unreleased GPU hardware. I lead performance testing for mainstream Radeon dGPU launches across a 22-node cluster, keep the Python benchmark automation honest, and drive the competitive analysis that tells the business how the silicon really stacks up.

Away from the benchmarks I carry a camera. I've been shooting since 2009 — street scenes, birds, landscapes, and the occasional macro — published under the name Arigraphy on Flickr.

I also run a small datacentre that lives in my home: an EPYC server with three GPUs serving open-weight AI models, storage, media, and git — all self-hosted, all open source. It's where I'm building MLOps fluency on ROCm, and I'm open to conversations about MLOps and AI-infrastructure roles. The full story is here.
